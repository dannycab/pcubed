A [1971 Ford Pinto](http://s.hswstatic.com/gif/1971-1980-ford-pinto-1971.jpg) is observed to moving with a velocity of $\langle 22.35,0,1.06\rangle\frac{m}{s}$. Determine the momentum of this sweet ride.

## Setup

You need to compute the momentum of a 1971 Ford Pinto using the information provided and any information that you can collect or assume.

### Facts

- The Ford Pinto is in motion
- It has a velocity, ${\overset{\rightarrow}{v}}_{car} = \langle 22.35,0,1.06\rangle\frac{m}{s}$.

### Lacking

- The mass of the Ford Pinto is not given, but can be [found online](http://lmgtfy.com/?q=mass+of+a+1971+ford+pinto+sedan) ($m_{car} = 884.05kg$).

### Approximations & Assumptions

- The Ford Pinto experiences several forces, but over the short time we are looking at it, it experiences no net force, so its velocity will remain unchanged.
- The velocity of the Ford Pinto is much less than the speed of light ($|{\overset{\rightarrow}{v}}_{car}| \ll c = 3.00 \times 10^{8}\frac{m}{s}$).

### Representations

- The momentum of the Ford Pinto is given by $\overset{\rightarrow}{p} = m\overset{\rightarrow}{v}$.

## Solution

We compute the momentum vector.

$$
{\overset{\rightarrow}{p}}_{car} = m_{car}{\overset{\rightarrow}{v}}_{car} = (884.05kg)\langle 22.35,0,1.06\rangle\frac{m}{s} = \langle 1.98 \times 10^{4},0,9.37 \times 10^{2}\rangle\frac{kg\ m}{s}
$$
