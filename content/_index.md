title: "Projects & Practices in Physics"
---

Projects & Practices in Physics (P³) is an introductory calculus-based physics
course redesigned to engage students through scientific practice. Instead of
traditional lectures, students work in groups on modeling projects, tackling
physics problems with both analytical methods and computational tools like
VPython, while instructors facilitate discussion and debate rather than
deliver material from the front of the room.

This site collects the P³ course notes, organized by week, migrated from the
original [MSU PERL wiki](https://www.msuperl.org/wikis/pcubed/doku.php?id=183_notes).

Course materials were developed by Michigan State University's [Physics
Education Research Lab](https://www.pa.msu.edu/) and Department of Physics
and Astronomy, drawing on physics education research and instructor feedback,
with inspiration from colleagues including Ruth Chabay and Bruce Sherwood.
